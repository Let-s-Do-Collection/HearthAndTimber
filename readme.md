Hearth & Timber adds a variety of simple yet intricate ways to add extra details to your already beautiful builds. Pull up the support beams, add the railings, trim your windows. That's personal flair right there.

<br>

Make your builds exactly how you want. Listen to your heart.

<br>

Hearth & Timber is Furniture's brother within the Let's Do collection and serves to detail the exterior and structural aesthetic of the interior while Furniture does the decor. Team work makes the dream work.

<br>

## Structural Integrity

<br>

- A variety of supports to make your structures look up to code  
- Some railings to fit on your balconies  
- Use an axe or a pickaxe to switch between stages  

<br>

If it falls, it was a design choice.

<br>

## Framing and Trimming

<br>

- A variety of frames that give that medieval frame effect, as well as frames that allow for new shapes and the creation of vertical slabs  
- Trims to give your windows and doors a modular finishing touch with the ability to create a windowsill as well by adding a flowerpot to the trim attached to your window  
- Framework, a weaker yet easier to craft version of scaffolding for vertical advantages  

<br>

It would seem that I've been... *framed.*

<br>

## Decorative Elements

<br>

- New wooden windows and panes  
- Sliding doors in three different sizes, adaptable by right-clicking them with any vanilla plank as well as an iron ingot  
- Thatch with four different controllable stages  
- Plaster, available in all 16 colors  
- A diverse assortment of wooden, terracotta, and patchwork shingles  
- A small variety of new stonetypes
